What this actually covers

Scheduled jobs, follow-ups, dashboards and document automation.

Scheduled jobs

Something runs every hour, day, or week without a person starting it. Pulling data, sending a report, checking a status, flagging when something needs attention.

Follow-ups and back-office work

The emails, reminders and document drafting that pile up because nobody has 20 minutes to do them right after the trigger event happens.

Dashboards and data pipelines

Public or internal data collected, cleaned, ranked, and put in front of you as a chart instead of a spreadsheet you have to build every time.

Browser and document automation

Repetitive steps across websites or paperwork, done the same correct way every time.

Frequently asked questions

The things people ask

Do I need to know what “automation” means technically?
No. Tell us what's repetitive and annoying. We work out whether it's a scheduled job, a dashboard, a form, or something else.
Is this the same as hiring an AI consultant?
Not exactly. AI is one tool in the kit, not the whole pitch. A good share of what we build isn't AI at all, it's ordinary software solving an ordinary problem.
What does this cost?
Depends what's being built. Small scheduled jobs and simple dashboards are the cheapest end. We quote after we know what you actually need, and the 5-Hour Audit is a fixed $999 if you want the full diagnosis first.
Do you maintain what you build, or just hand it over?
Both are on the table. Some clients want it handed off, some want us to keep running it. We agree on that before we start.
What if my business is too small for this?
If you're the one still doing the repetitive task yourself at 9pm, you're exactly who this is for.
