Three of them, one job each.

They speak only when mentioned, reply in threads, and treat everything they read as data rather than instructions.

Foreman

Runs the room

Posts a real standup: shipped, in progress with owners, blocked and on whom. Turns vague asks into tasks with done-conditions, and says "I don't know" instead of inventing status.

Gauge

Reads the diff

Every finding names what breaks and the exact input that breaks it. One clear verdict at the end, and it flags auth, payment and deletion paths even when the code looks fine.

Verdict

Rules on "done"

Marks every requirement shown or merely claimed, probes the edges nobody mentioned, then rules SHIP or NOT DONE. "I tested it and it works" is not evidence.

In the hive in about a minute.

No account, no signup, nothing metered by us. You bring your own harness and model key.

  1. Download dev-crew.team.json from the repo.
  2. In Buzz Desktop: Agents → Agent teams → + → Import, then confirm the preview lists Foreman, Gauge and Verdict.
  3. Agent teams → Dev Crew → Deploy to channel, pick your channel, role bot.
  4. Mention one of them. Buzz mints each a fresh keypair on import, so your identity never travels inside the file.
