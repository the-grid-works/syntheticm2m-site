LingoFlyer

LingoFlyer, a language learning game you fly through

Steer a wingsuit through gates shaped like the right translation, and clear 20 languages one city at a time.

Free to download

How it works

How it works

Answer with your wings

Every gate is a vocabulary question. Steer with a finger or by tilting your phone to fly through the correct translation and keep your streak alive.

A journey through 20 languages

Each language is a country with 20 cities and 500 phrases people actually say. Clear a city to open the route to the next one.

Race and review

Race three rival flyers through storm clouds, or work through short lessons that quiz both directions and bring back the phrases you missed.

Private by default

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your progress stays on your device.

Who it is for

Anyone who wants to pick up everyday phrases in a new language without flashcards or a classroom, in short sessions on a phone.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which languages does LingoFlyer teach?
20 in all: Spanish (Spain and Latin America), French, Portuguese, German, Italian, Russian, Arabic, Hindi, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Farsi, Bosnian, Urdu, Bengali, Swahili, Indonesian, Vietnamese and English. You can learn from any of them.
Do I need an account to play?
No. There is no account, no ads and no tracking. Your progress is saved on your device.
How do I play?
Steer with a finger or by tilting your phone to fly through the gate with the right translation. Miss it and your streak resets.
Is there a cost?
LingoFlyer is free to download and play.
Does the game ever end?
Each language has 20 cities to clear. Finish a language and you have completed its journey.

Take LingoFlyer with you.
