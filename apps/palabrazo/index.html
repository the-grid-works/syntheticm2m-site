Palabrazo

Palabrazo, a daily Spanish word game

Every night a five-letter Spanish word is hidden. Guess it from the color clues, then share your grid without giving away the word.

Free to download

Palabrazo Plus is an optional one-time purchase that unlocks five extra ways to play.

How it works

How it works

One word, six tries

Every night a five-letter Spanish word is hidden. Type your guesses and read the color clues; accents never count against you.

Build your constellation

Every night you solve becomes a star, with stats, your guess distribution and your streak, and no guilt when you miss a day.

Share without spoiling

Share your color grid in one tap. It never gives away the word.

Palabrazo Plus

A one-time purchase adds five more ways to play: Aprende, where the clue is the meaning; Duo, two words at once; Cadena, where each word starts with the last letter of the one before; Vocales, vowels only; and Archivo, every past night plus another word whenever you want.

Who it is for

Spanish learners and native speakers who want a two-minute daily word puzzle, using everyday Spanish from Latin America and Spain.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I play Palabrazo?
Every night a five-letter Spanish word is hidden. You get six tries, and the color clues show how close each guess is.
Do accents count against me?
No. Accents never count against your guess.
What is Palabrazo Plus?
A one-time purchase that adds five extra ways to play: Aprende, Duo, Cadena, Vocales and Archivo, which unlocks every past night plus another word whenever you want.
Is the word list only from Spain?
No. It uses everyday Spanish from both Latin America and Spain, with no proper nouns and no plurals.
Is it free?
Yes, the daily word and your constellation are free. Palabrazo Plus is an optional one-time purchase.
