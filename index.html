Web apps & SaaS

Arbitration Desk

For a used-car dealer, this checks an auction's arbitration offer against the auction's own written policy and shows exactly where it is wrong.

Problem
Used-car dealers get lowball arbitration offers from wholesale auctions.
Built
A web tool that reads the deal paperwork, checks the offer against the auction's own published policy for that sale date, and writes a rebuttal letter in English and Spanish.
Result
Live, free case check. A paid plan takes payments.

Data pipelines & dashboards

Alt-Data Signal Hub

For an investor or analyst, this pulls public market signals into one dashboard that refreshes itself every day.

Problem
Useful market signals are scattered across public sources.
Built
A pipeline that fuses insider buying clusters, federal contract awards, short volume trends, severe weather alerts and news, ranked across 350+ tickers, with interactive charts.
Result
Live dashboard that refreshes itself daily with nobody touching it. Data only, no advice.
